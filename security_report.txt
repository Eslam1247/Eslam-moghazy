# UX Research Security & Privacy Report - Eslam Moghazy

At the core of Eslam Moghazy's UX research methodology is a commitment to participant privacy, data security, and ethical research conduct. This report outlines the standard security measures and ethical guidelines followed in every project.

## 1. Participant Anonymity & Privacy
- **Consent First**: All participants provide informed consent before any research session begins.
- **Data Anonymization**: Participant names and identifying information are removed from research synthesis and shared reports to protect privacy.
- **GDPR & PII Compliance**: Strict adherence to Personal Identifiable Information (PII) protection standards in accordance with regional regulations.

## 2. Secure Data Storage
- **Encrypted Channels**: All research raw data (video, audio, transcripts) is stored on encrypted, password-protected cloud platforms.
- **Limited Access**: Research databases are restricted to core team members only, following the principle of least privilege.
- **Data Lifecycle**: Raw participant data is securely deleted after the completion of the project unless explicit long-term consent is obtained.

## 3. Platform Security
- **Secure Tools**: Only industry-vetted research platforms (e.g., Maze, Dovetail, UserTesting) with validated security certifications are utilized.
- **VPN usage**: Research analysis and data management are conducted over secure VPN connections where necessary.

## 4. Ethical Conduct
- **Bias Mitigation**: Implementing diverse participant pools to minimize algorithmic and researcher bias.
- **Transparent Communication**: Participants are briefed on how their data will be used and given the right to withdraw at any time.

Eslam Moghazy ensures that your users' data is treated with the highest level of respect and security throughout the design thinking process.
